# task_06 — Reference solution (DRAFT, not yet verified)

**Scenario:** redundancy; March 1, 2017 to September 30, 2025 = 8 years 7 months; monthly rate ₱32,500.

## Step 1: Years of service

8 years + 7 months. 7 months is at least 6 months, so it counts as 1 whole year → **9 years**.

## Step 2: Separation pay (redundancy)

- One month pay × years = ₱32,500 × 9 = ₱292,500
- Minimum = one month pay = ₱32,500
- Greater of the two = **₱292,500.00**

```
FINAL ANSWER: ₱292,500.00
```
