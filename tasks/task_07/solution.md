# task_07 — Reference solution (DRAFT, not yet verified)

**Scenario:** retrenchment to prevent losses; July 1, 2024 to September 30, 2025 = 1 year 3 months;
monthly rate ₱27,400.

## Step 1: Years of service

1 year + 3 months. 3 months is less than 6 months, so it is not counted → **1 year**.

## Step 2: Separation pay (retrenchment)

- One-half month pay = ₱27,400 ÷ 2 = ₱13,700
- Half-month pay × years = ₱13,700 × 1 = ₱13,700
- Minimum = one month pay = ₱27,400
- Greater of the two = **₱27,400.00** (the minimum applies)

```
FINAL ANSWER: ₱27,400.00
```

**Known weakness of this task:** two other mistakes happen to land on the correct ₱27,400.00.
They are rounding the 3 months up (2 years × ₱13,700) and using full-month pay per year (1 × ₱27,400).
This is unavoidable here. Whenever the minimum is higher than half-month × years, years is at most 1,
so full-month × years can never exceed the minimum. This task therefore mainly tests the minimum
rule. Read transcripts, not just pass/fail, for this one.
