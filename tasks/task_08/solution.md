# task_08 — Reference solution (DRAFT, not yet verified)

**Scenario:** retrenchment to prevent losses; May 1, 2018 to September 30, 2025 = 7 years 5 months;
monthly rate ₱24,600.

## Step 1: Years of service

7 years + 5 months. 5 months is less than 6 months, so it is not counted → **7 years**.

## Step 2: Separation pay (retrenchment)

- One-half month pay = ₱24,600 ÷ 2 = ₱12,300
- Half-month pay × years = ₱12,300 × 7 = ₱86,100
- Minimum = one month pay = ₱24,600
- Greater of the two = **₱86,100.00**

```
FINAL ANSWER: ₱86,100.00
```
